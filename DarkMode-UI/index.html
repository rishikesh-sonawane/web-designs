FAQ

Common questions
How does the free tier work?

The Starter plan is free forever with up to 10,000 events per month, 3 team members, and 7-day data retention. No credit card required. Upgrade anytime when you need more capacity.

Can I migrate from another analytics tool?

Yes. Lumen supports one-click imports from Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, PostHog, and Segment. Historical data is preserved and immediately queryable in your new dashboard.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We are SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Business plans include dedicated infrastructure and custom data residency options.

Do you offer an API?

Yes. Lumen provides a comprehensive REST API and real-time WebSocket API for building custom dashboards, triggering workflows, and integrating analytics data into your own applications.

What happens when I exceed my event limit?

We never cut off your data. If you exceed your monthly event limit, Lumen continues collecting and we notify you. You can upgrade your plan at any time — retroactive billing is prorated automatically.
